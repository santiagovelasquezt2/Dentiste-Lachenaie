# Welcome to our dental clinic in Lachenaie

## Where we care about your oral health

Established in 2000, our clinic offers complete dental care for the whole family, for both children and adults.

We offer a full range of dental care and treatment services, from preventive dentistry to dental surgery.

# A dedicated and human team

## **Skills, expertise, understanding**

At Vaillancourt St-Onge Dental Centre, you’ll find **two highly qualified and dedicated dental teams** (dentists and support staff) to provide you with the best dental experience in terms of **gentleness** and **understanding** of your needs.

You’ll be greeted by **smiling and enthusiastic** staff. At Vaillancourt St-Onge Dental Centre, we offer an **unparalleled** level of professional competence.
